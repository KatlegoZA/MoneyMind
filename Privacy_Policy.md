1. Introduction

MoneyMind ("MoneyMind", "we", "us" or "our") is a personal financial management application designed to help users better understand their personal finances, manage budgets, track income and expenses, set savings goals, and perform financial calculations.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your Personal Information.

This Privacy Policy explains how MoneyMind collects, uses, stores, shares and protects Personal Information when you use the MoneyMind mobile application and related services.

MoneyMind processes Personal Information in accordance with applicable privacy and data-protection laws, including the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA") in South Africa and, where applicable, the General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR").

For purposes of POPIA, the responsible party is:

Responsible Party: Saphir Technologies

Country: South Africa

Privacy Email: privacy@saphirtech.co.za

2. Information We Collect

The Personal Information MoneyMind processes depends on the features you use.

2.1. Account and Authentication Information

When you register for or use a MoneyMind account, information may include:

email address;
password or authentication credentials;
Firebase user identifier;
email-verification status;

Users should never enter this information into MoneyMind.

Depending on the features used, this information may include:

Budget information

Savings information

repayment scenarios.
Where a feature operates only as a calculator, information may be used temporarily to perform the calculation and may not necessarily be stored in your MoneyMind account.

Where storage or synchronisation functionality is enabled, MoneyMind may store information in its Firebase database. MoneyMind does not itself provide banking, lending, investment, financial-advice or credit-bureau services merely because users enter financial information into the App.

3. Biometric Authentication

MoneyMind may allow users to use supported device authentication methods such as:

fingerprint authentication;
facial authentication;
another biometric authentication method supported by the device; or
the device PIN, password or pattern.
The operating system and your device's biometric security handle biometric authentication. MoneyMind receives the result of the authentication process, such as whether authentication was successful. MoneyMind does not intentionally collect or store your fingerprint image, facial image, fingerprint template or facial-recognition template. If biometric login is enabled, MoneyMind may store information on your device that is necessary to facilitate subsequent authentication. You can stop using biometric login where the functionality is made available through the App or your device settings.

7. Firebase and Third-Party Service Providers

MoneyMind uses services supplied by Google Firebase, including services such as:

Google generally acts as a data processor or service provider for customer data supplied to Firebase, subject to Google's applicable service and data-processing terms.

MoneyMind may also rely on operating-system providers, hosting providers and technical-service providers where necessary to provide App functionality.

MoneyMind uses Google AdMob, a service provided by Google, to display advertisements within the application. Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and process certain information, such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, general location information, advertisement interactions, and other usage information, for purposes including displaying advertisements, measuring advertising performance, preventing fraud, and, where permitted and consented to, personalising advertisements. The collection and processing of this information by Google is subject to Google’s applicable privacy policies and advertising practices.

MoneyMind may offer paid subscriptions or other in-app purchases that provide access to additional features or benefits, including an advertisement-free experience where applicable. On Android devices, Google Play processes and manages subscriptions and in-app purchases. Google processes payments and provides the infrastructure for subscription billing, renewals, cancellations, and applicable refunds under Google Play’s terms and policies.

MoneyMind does not directly collect, process, or store users’ full credit card, debit card, or banking details used to make purchases through Google Play. Payment information provided during a Google Play transaction is handled by Google and its authorised payment providers. MoneyMind may receive limited information from Google about a purchase or subscription, such as whether a subscription is active, the product or subscription purchased, transaction information, and information needed to verify entitlement to paid features.

Users can manage or cancel their MoneyMind subscriptions through their Google Play account. Cancelling a subscription generally prevents future renewals but does not necessarily terminate access to benefits already paid for immediately; access may continue until the end of the applicable billing period, subject to Google Play’s terms.

MoneyMind may also use or introduce additional third-party services, including analytics providers, crash-reporting services, bank-account aggregation services, authentication providers, cloud services, or other integrations. Where these services materially affect how personal information is collected, used, processed, or shared, this Privacy Policy will be updated where required.

Third-party services used by MoneyMind may operate under their own privacy policies and terms. Users are encouraged to review the relevant third-party policies, including Google’s Privacy Policy and Google Play terms, to understand how those providers handle personal information.
